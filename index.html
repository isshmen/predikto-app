Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about PREDIKTO.

Is real money required to play?

No. PREDIKTO is a free social betting platform using internal tokens called Predikto. You receive 100 free tokens upon registration and can earn more via referrals.

How is the live Bitcoin price determined?

The live price is fetched continuously from public Binance market data feeds, ensuring transparent real-time offset calculations.

How does the multi-prediction feature work?

You can submit multiple target price predictions for Draw #1 simultaneously. Each prediction costs 10 Predikto tokens from your balance.

How do I earn referral tokens?

Copy your unique referral link from the "Referrals" tab. When a new user registers through your link and places their first prediction, 100 bonus tokens are credited to your balance.
